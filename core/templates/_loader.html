{% comment %} MedlynAI loader — one consistent "please wait" for the whole app. Shows automatically when: • a link or form opens a new page (incl. JS redirects) → bar at 150ms, card at 700ms • a fetch() started by a click/submit is slow → bar at 300ms, card at 1.5s Further clicks are blocked while a page is opening, so doctors can't double-submit. Opt out: add data-no-loader to a link, form or any parent element, or call fetch(url, { loader: false }) Message: data-loader-message="Saving bill…" on a link, button or form Manual: const t = MedlynLoader.show('Generating PDF…'); …; MedlynLoader.hide(t); {% endcomment %}
